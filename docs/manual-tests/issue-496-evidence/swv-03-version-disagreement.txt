Conclusion: in both cases the installed-binary query (`karst --version`)
reflects the replacement immediately, while the running-daemon query (`karst
version`, served over the daemon's live control socket) keeps reporting the
pre-upgrade build until the daemon process actually restarts. The two
disagree for exactly the window between binary replacement and restart, and
agree again immediately after restart. (SWV-03: Pass.)
